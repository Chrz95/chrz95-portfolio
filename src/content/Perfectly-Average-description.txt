APP TITLE
Perfectly Average

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Turn numbers into the perfect average. Calm puzzles with clever twists.

FULL DESCRIPTION
A few numbers. One target. A surprisingly satisfying puzzle.

Perfectly Average is a thoughtful number puzzle game where every move changes the average. Add, subtract, double and halve your tiles, then discover new twists as you work toward the target. Simple rules lead to clever solutions—and that lovely moment when everything clicks.

100 LEVELS, 20 CHAPTERS
Start with approachable puzzles and progress to harder combinations of fractions, fixed tiles, limited operations and exact tile arrangements. Earn stars, improve your best moves and revisit your favourite boards.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
Take on 30 Mastery challenges and 16 linked-tile puzzles. Return for a daily challenge, tackle a five-puzzle course or keep exploring in Endless mode. Share the device for a friendly two-player, pass-and-play match.

MAKE YOUR OWN PUZZLES
Experiment in Puzzle Lab, check whether your creation has a solution and share it using a puzzle code. Save custom boards and challenge yourself to find a better route.

CELEBRATE YOUR PROGRESS
Complete chapters to reveal distinctive artwork and unlock new colour palettes. Collect cosmetics and give the game a look that feels like yours.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Keep the level timer on for extra pressure or turn it off in Settings. Use undo and progressive hints when you need a fresh perspective, and replay a winning solution to understand how it worked.

A CALM PLACE TO THINK
Enjoy clean visuals, gentle music and satisfying sounds. Adjust audio, haptics and motion to suit your preferences.

Play puzzles offline. Optional rewarded ads can offer an extra chance when available; there are no banner ads or automatic interstitial breaks. No account is required.

Find the balance. Make every move count.
