# Privacy Policy for Perfectly Average

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

Perfectly Average is provided by **Christos Zacharioudakis** (“we”, “us” or “our”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **chrz1995@gmail.com**.

## Information stored on your device

Perfectly Average stores game information locally on your device, including:

- Level progress, stars, scores and best moves.
- Saved puzzles, custom puzzles and unfinished sessions.
- Unlocked cosmetics and selected appearance.
- Sound, music, timer, vibration and motion preferences.

This information lets the game remember your progress and preferences. The game does not upload these save files to servers operated by us.

Perfectly Average does not require an account. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

## Advertising and third-party services

Perfectly Average uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Watching an advertisement is not required to play the puzzles.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share information such as:

- Your IP address, which may indicate your approximate location.
- Device and advertising identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available.
- App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
- Diagnostic and performance information.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The information processed depends on your privacy choices, device settings and the advertising services in use.

The game requests non-personalized advertisements. Non-personalized advertising may still involve identifiers or other information used for measurement, frequency limits and fraud prevention.

Google’s handling of information is described in its policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## Privacy choices

Where applicable, the game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices before requesting an advertisement.

When available, you can review your choices through **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.

You can also use your Android device settings to reset or delete your advertising ID. Available controls vary by Android version.

You may decline optional advertisements and continue playing. The advertising consent service may communicate with Google to determine which privacy choices apply, even if you do not watch an advertisement.

## Puzzle sharing and clipboard use

When you choose to copy a puzzle code or result, the game places that text on your device’s clipboard.

Perfectly Average does not automatically publish your puzzles or results. If you share them through another application, that application’s privacy policy applies.

## Information you send to us

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and any screenshots or diagnostic logs.

We use this information to respond to your request, troubleshoot problems and maintain the game. Please avoid including passwords or unnecessary personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until it is reset or removed. You can delete locally stored information by clearing the app’s storage through Android settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local files; device backup and restore services may retain separate copies under their own settings.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Google determines the retention of information it processes under its own policies. Removing the game’s local data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle. Google states that information collected by its advertising SDK is encrypted in transit.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

## Children’s privacy

We do not ask players to provide their name, date of birth or contact details to play the game.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, contact **chrz1995@gmail.com** so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its processing, request a copy, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request concerning information held by us, contact **chrz1995@gmail.com**. For information processed by Google, please also use the privacy controls and contact options described in Google’s policies.

You may have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
